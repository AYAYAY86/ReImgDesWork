1. Mouse Cursor – Process & Reflection

The cursor is more than just a pointer; it shapes how users and graphic designers interact with digital tools. It acts as a kind of prosthesis, the mouse translates physical movement into digital action through x and y coordinates, enabling orientation and navigation in virtual space. It becomes a visible extension of the hand, linking gesture, thought, and screen. This creates a tactile dialogue between user and interface, where movement and representation are closely intertwined.

On trackpads and touchscreens, gestures like swiping and zooming mimic physical actions, making the interface feel more immediate and responsive. The cursor is less visible in these contexts but still plays a key role, particularly in graphic design workflows where pointing, selecting, and manipulating elements remain central tasks.

2. GUI – Process & Reflection

The Graphical User Interface (GUI) revolutionized Human Computer Interaction, enabling visual interaction with computers. GUIs provide visual representations, interactivity, simplified controls, and immediate feedback, making computers accessible to a broader audience.

Through windows, icons, menus, and pointers, GUIs allow users to interact with digital content intuitively. This chapter explores the evolution and impact of GUIs on design workflows and user experience.
